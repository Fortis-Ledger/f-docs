# Workplace Policy — FortisArena

> FortisArena is a hybrid-first gaming platform built on creativity, performance, and flexibility.

---

## 🏢 Our Work Model

**Policy Type:** Hybrid

At FortisArena, we operate on a **hybrid work model** — our team has the freedom to work remotely while coming together in-person for key sprints, launches, and collaboration sessions. We believe great games and platforms are built by energized, focused people — not by where they sit.

---

## ⏰ On-Site Expectations

**Typical Time On-Site:** Flexible

We don't enforce rigid in-office days. Instead, we align around **project milestones and team needs**. In-person presence is encouraged for:

- Major product planning sessions
- Team onboarding and training
- Launch weeks and crunch periods
- Creative brainstorming workshops

Outside of these, team members have full flexibility to choose where they work best.

---

## 🌐 Remote Work Guidelines

- All team members are equipped with the tools needed to collaborate effectively from anywhere.
- Core working hours are **12:00 PM – 6:00 PM PKT** to ensure team overlap.
- Async communication is encouraged for non-urgent tasks via our internal channels.
- Performance is measured by **output and impact**, not hours logged.
