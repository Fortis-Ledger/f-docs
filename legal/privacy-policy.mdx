## 3. Meta / Facebook Login and Platform Data

FortisArena may provide users with the option to authenticate or connect their FortisArena account using Meta/Facebook Login.

### 3.1 Information We May Receive

When you choose Meta/Facebook Login and authorize our application, Meta may provide information permitted by the permissions approved for our application. Depending on the permissions actually requested and authorized, this may include:

- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture
- Meta/Facebook user identifier
- Other information specifically made available through an authorized permission

We do **not** claim access to information that our application does not request or that Meta does not make available to the application.

### 3.2 How We Use Meta/Facebook Data

Information received through Meta/Facebook Login is used only as reasonably necessary to:

1. Authenticate you and help you sign in to FortisArena
2. Create, connect, and maintain your FortisArena account
3. Provide account and profile functionality
4. Maintain account security and prevent unauthorized access
5. Provide support relating to your FortisArena account
6. Operate features for which the relevant information is necessary and authorized

We do not sell information obtained through Meta/Facebook Login.

We do not use information obtained through Meta/Facebook Login for purposes unrelated to providing or operating the FortisArena services described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not request Meta/Facebook permissions merely to obtain information that is not needed for a FortisArena feature.

### 3.3 Meta/Facebook Data Sharing

We may use infrastructure and service providers that process information on our behalf, such as hosting, database, security, authentication, and other technical service providers necessary to operate FortisArena.

We do not sell Meta/Facebook information or provide it to third parties for independent advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of FortisArena and its users.

### 3.4 Meta/Facebook Data Retention

We retain information obtained through Meta/Facebook Login only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant FortisArena account or service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

If you disconnect Meta/Facebook Login or request deletion of your FortisArena account, we will process the relevant information for deletion in accordance with the account deletion provisions of this Privacy Policy, subject to information that we are legally permitted or required to retain.

### 3.5 Meta/Facebook Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your FortisArena account and associated personal information at any time by contacting:

**privacy@fortisarena.io**

You may also use our dedicated data deletion page:

**https://docs.fortisarena.io/legal/data-deletion**

A deletion request may require reasonable verification to protect the account from unauthorized requests.

Where applicable, deletion will include information associated with your FortisArena account that we are not legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

If you used Meta/Facebook Login, requesting deletion of your FortisArena account will also initiate the deletion process for information received from Meta/Facebook that is associated with your FortisArena account, subject to applicable legal, security, and fraud-prevention retention requirements.

## 4. FortisArena Wallet System

### 4.1 UID System (Internal Identifiers)

FortisArena uses a **UID (User Identifier)** system for certain internal transactions and platform functionality:

- **Your UID:** `FORTIS-UID-XXXX` format
- **Used for:** Internal transfers, tips, rewards, and related platform functionality
- **Visibility:** May be publicly visible on your profile where the relevant feature is enabled

### 4.2 Wallet Security (MPC Technology)

Where FortisArena provides MPC-based wallet functionality:

| Feature | How It Works |
|:--------|:-------------|
| **No Seed Phrases** | Account recovery through supported account verification methods |
| **Key Distribution** | Private-key material may be distributed across secure infrastructure |
| **Platform Custody** | FortisArena or its designated infrastructure may secure wallet key material where applicable |
| **Recovery** | Supported account recovery and verification mechanisms |

<Warning>
**Important:** FortisArena wallet functionality may differ from a traditional self-custodial wallet. Users should review the applicable wallet terms before using wallet-related features.
</Warning>

### 4.3 Blockchain Data

For supported on-chain transactions:

- **Public Blockchain Data:** Wallet addresses and transaction hashes may be recorded on a public blockchain and may be permanently visible.
- **Internal Ledger:** UID-to-UID transfers may be recorded in FortisArena's internal systems.
- **User Control:** Where withdrawals are supported, users may withdraw assets to supported external wallet addresses.

Blockchain data that has already been recorded on a public blockchain generally cannot be deleted or altered by FortisArena.
