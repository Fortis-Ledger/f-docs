## 🔄 Seasonal Reset (Quarterly)

### What Resets
- ❌ Points above rank minimum (70% kept, 30% reset)

### What Stays
- ✅ Rank level (if active)
- ✅ Followers
- ✅ Content history
- ✅ Badges earned

### Activity Requirements to Maintain Rank

| Rank | Minimum Activity/Quarter |
|:-----|:-------------------------|
| 1 | 3 posts OR 20 engagements |
| 2 | 5 posts OR 50 engagements |
| 3 | 2 active communities OR 100 engagements |
| 4 | 1 verified community OR governance participation |
