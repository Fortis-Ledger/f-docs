## ✅ Verification Levels

FortisArena has **two verification tiers** based on what you want to do:

### Level 1: Email Verified (Default)

**Required for:**
- ✅ Creating an account
- ✅ Browsing social platform
- ✅ Earning social points
- ✅ Rank progression (0-4)
- ✅ Basic wallet features
- ✅ Free tournaments viewing

**How to get:**
- Verify email OR
- Use Google/Apple OAuth (pre-verified) OR
- Verify phone number

### Level 2: KYC Verified (For Competitive Features)

**Required for:**
- 🏆 Tournament participation
- 💰 Earning real prizes (FRT/FPT)
- 🤝 Joining squads
- 🏰 Joining guilds
- 💸 High-value withdrawals
- 🎮 Competitive matchmaking

**How to get:**
- Submit government ID
- Take selfie for matching
- Wait for approval (instant to 24 hours)

## 🚫 Common Issues

### Didn't Receive Verification Email

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5 minutes
3. Click "Resend" on signup page
4. Try different email provider
5. Contact support if still missing

### "Email Already Exists"

- Try logging in instead
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed
- Check if you used Google OAuth previously

### Can't Complete KYC

| Issue | Solution |
|:------|:---------|
| ID rejected | Ensure clear photo, good lighting |
| Selfie doesn't match | Remove glasses, ensure good lighting |
| Country not supported | Check [supported countries list](/tournaments/verification) |
| Under 18 | Must be 18+ for KYC verification |
